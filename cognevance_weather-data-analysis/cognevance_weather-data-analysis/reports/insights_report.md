# Weather Data Analysis - Insights Report

**Period:** 01 Jan 2019 - 31 Dec 2023 (1,818 daily records)

## 1. Data cleaning summary
| Step | Result |
|---|---|
| Raw rows | 1,841 |
| Rows with unparseable dates removed | 9 |
| Duplicate dates removed | 14 |
| Impossible / sensor-error values set to missing | 19 |
| Missing cells before imputation | 244 |
| Missing cells after imputation | 0 |
| Clean rows | 1,818 |

Continuous variables were filled by time-based interpolation; missing rainfall was treated as 0 mm.

## 2. Key findings
* **Temperature:** Jun is the hottest month (mean 34.7 °C, average daily high 39.9 °C); Dec is the coldest (15.2 °C). Hottest single day: 05 Jun 2023 at 45.2 °C.
* **Rainfall:** Aug is the wettest month (about 253 mm on average). The monsoon season (Jul-Sep) delivers **80%** of total rainfall.
* **Humidity:** highest in Aug (82%), lowest in Apr (37%).
* **Seasonal contrast (mean temperature):** Monsoon 27.9 °C, Post-monsoon 17.1 °C, Summer 31.6 °C, Winter 18.1 °C.
* **Relationships:** temperature vs humidity r = 0.27; rainfall vs humidity r = 0.59. Rainy days are noticeably more humid.
* **Year-on-year:** fitted trend in annual mean temperature is -0.03 °C/year over 5 years (too short a window to say anything about climate change).

## 5. Recommendations
* Schedule outdoor events and travel in **Oct-Mar** when it is dry and moderate; avoid the Jun heat peak and the Aug peak rainfall.
* Agriculture: plan sowing and irrigation around the monsoon concentration (80% of rain in three months); store water for the dry months.
* Energy: cooling demand peaks around Jun; heating demand is minimal in this climate.

## 6. Limitations
The dataset is synthetic (generated by `src/generate_dataset.py`) to mimic a north-Indian climate, so figures illustrate the method rather than real observations. Swap in a Kaggle dataset to analyse real data.
